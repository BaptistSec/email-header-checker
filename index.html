email-header-check

Email header checker

Paste the full headers of a message (in Gmail: Show original; in Outlook: Message options). It reads the SPF, DKIM and DMARC results your own mail provider recorded. Everything runs in this page. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

It reports what the Authentication-Results header says. It does not verify signatures itself, and a passing result does not prove a message is safe. Only trust headers added by your own provider (the topmost one).
